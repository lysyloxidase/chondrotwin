## 5. Deviations and diagnostics (post hoc — not part of the registered result)

* **GSE114007 is not analysable as registered.** The files named "normalized counts" contain negative values (39.5 % of all entries in the normal file, 37.1 % in the OA file; negative entries normal/OA: SOX9 0/0, RUNX2 1/0, MMP13 5/0, CDKN2A 16/14), i.e. they are batch-corrected values, not counts. Ratios of mean CPM are then undefined for MMP13 and CDKN2A (NaN intervals) and not meaningful for the others. The registered secondary result is therefore declared invalid. A valid analysis needs `GSE114007_raw_counts.xlsx`, which was not downloaded.
* **GSE57218 probe intensity** (percentile of the probe's mean among all probes): SOX9 99th, RUNX2 69th, MMP13 77th, CDKN2A 56th. The CDKN2A probe is close to the array background, so its "no change" is weak evidence; the narrow interval reflects low signal rather than precise measurement.
* Direction of the within-joint MMP13 change: lower in OA-affected than in preserved cartilage of the same joint in GSE57218, while the model (any stage) predicts higher catabolic drive in damaged tissue. Whether lesioned end-stage cartilage should be compared with the model's "damaged" tissue is itself an assumption of the protocol.

## 7. Conclusions

* **The stage-3 hypotheses were not supported.** Neither heterogeneity of the switch nor limited senescence competence improved the calibration on GSE104782, so the frozen stage-3 model is the stage-2 model; the best heterogeneous set also does worse on the validation patients and on every external dataset (exploratory).
* **Pre-registered primary test (GSE57218, 33 joint-matched pairs): stage 2 = 3 2 / 4, stage 1 0 / 4.** The selected model is consistent with no material within-joint change of SOX9 and RUNX2, and it fails for MMP13 (data lower in OA-affected tissue, model higher) and for CDKN2A (data flat but near background; model strongly higher).
* **Overall, across stages:** the revisions kept so far (stage 2) improved the fit to held-out patients, to SOX9 in both external datasets and to RUNX2 in GSE57218 (not in GSE152805, where intact model tissue still has no RUNX2+ cells). None fixed senescence — the model's p16 rise starts earlier and saturates higher than in the validation patients and the external data — or catabolism in end-stage lesions. These are the two places where the model's logic, not its parameters, is most likely wrong.
* **What would change the picture:** longitudinal OAI data (time scale, tissue), GSE114007 raw counts (a valid secondary test), and a senescence readout better than one transcript (e.g. a multi-gene SASP / senescence score).
