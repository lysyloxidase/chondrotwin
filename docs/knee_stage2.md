## 1. Revised rules and calibration (GSE104782, patients OA1–OA5)

Each revision answers a stage-1 falsification; with default values the ABM is the 0.2–0.4 model (bit-identical published time courses, `tests/test_abm.py`):

* `damage_D50` — senescence hazard scaled by accumulated overload D / (D + D50) (cumulative damage);
* `c_over_max` — largest catabolic bias that strain alone can produce (below the saddle-node 0.51, overload alone cannot flip a SOX9-high cell; inflammation is then required);
* `k_cat` — switch-independent catabolism (NF-κB / HIF-2α → MMP13): degradation ∝ (R + k_cat · overload);
* `baseline_runx2` — fraction of RUNX2-high cells at the start (stage-0 cartilage of OA knees has RUNX2+ cells).

## 4. Conclusions

* **Cell states, held-out patients: improved.** Validation loss 798 → 88. Two of the revisions were selected: strain alone can no longer flip a SOX9-high cell (c_over_max = 0.15 < 0.51) and catabolism partly bypasses the switch (k_cat = 0.5). Mean SOX9 activity in the most degraded tissue is now 41 % of intact tissue (stage-1 model 3 %; data, validation patients, 60 %).
* **Senescence timing: not improved.** The cumulative-damage hazard was not selected (D50 = 0). Stage 1 vs 0 p16 log-ratio: data -0.27, stage-1 model +1.32, stage-2 model +1.63. In the model, degrading tissue stays overloaded long enough to reach the damage thresholds tested (D50 up to 1000 overload-days); senescence then saturates at 100 %, which also fails the independent test (model 7.0× vs data 1.7–2.5×).
* **Independent pre-registered test: 1 / 4 (stage 2) vs 0 / 4 (stage 1).** The direction of change is right for every marker in both models; magnitudes fail because intact model tissue has no RUNX2+ cells and senescence saturates.
* **Tissue: unchanged.** The lateral plate is still predicted not to erode where a small loss is observed. The stage-2 model has a latent period (no erosion at any stress within 2 simulated years) followed by rapid, threshold-like erosion, so its tissue prediction now depends on the unknown time scale.
* **Next (stage 3).** A RUNX2+ subpopulation in intact cartilage (cell-to-cell heterogeneity of the switch rather than a uniform initial state), senescence that is not an absorbing fate of every overloaded cell (e.g. clearance or a damage-independent cap), and longitudinal OAI data to fix the time scale.
