## 5. Conclusions

* **Identifiability:** the strain-only switch bias `c_over_max` is confined to [0, 0.2] — below the saddle-node in the whole region, so 'strain alone cannot flip the switch' is a robust conclusion. `k_cat` is not identified (region spans [0, 1], including 0): the stage-2 claim that catabolism partly bypasses the switch is withdrawn as a separate finding.
* **Robustness:** stage 2 beats stage 1 on held-out patients in 48 of 48 analysis universes (median loss ratio 4.9).
* **Structural failure confirmed:** near-complete senescence in degraded tissue holds across the whole parameter region; no parameter choice within this model structure can fix it.
* **For the paper:** the scorecard above is the honest summary — the revisions improve cell-state predictions on held-out and independent data, the tissue-level and senescence predictions do not improve, and the synovial hypothesis could not be supported with open data.
