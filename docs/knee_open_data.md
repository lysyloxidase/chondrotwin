## 2. Mechanical bridge: time averaging

Biphasic confined compression (τ = 4500 s) under the measured gait waveform for 3τ versus a constant stress equal to its time average: surface displacement differs by **1.3 %**. The ABM is therefore driven by a daily effective stress σ = κ · (cycle-mean compartment force) / (plate area), with κ calibrated below.

## 6. What the data say about the model

* **Supported.** Medial-predominant loss follows from the measured in vivo medial force share without being fitted; a valgus load distribution reverses it. The direction of the cell-state changes agrees with the data: MMP13+ and p16+ cells increase in the most degraded cartilage (stage 4 / 0 intervals exclude 1); RUNX2+ cells change in the same direction (interval includes 1).
* **Not supported.** (i) Senescence and the SOX9 → RUNX2 switch occur far too early and too completely in the ABM: the data show p16, RUNX2 and MMP13 detection without a clear rise up to stage 3 and SOX9 retained in > 90 % of cells at stage 4. (ii) The tissue response is all-or-none (a sharp stress threshold), so the model predicts essentially no lateral loss where a small one is observed. (iii) Early-OA thickening (KL 2) is absent from the model.
* **Implication for stage 2.** Replace the instantaneous overload-driven senescence hazard by a cumulative-damage hazard, allow graded SOX9/RUNX2 co-expression (lower cooperativity or a third, intermediate state), add matrix swelling, and calibrate against longitudinal OAI data (requires NDA access).

## 7. Assumptions (each one is a place where this analysis can be wrong)

1. Loads come from total knee replacements (no menisci, no cruciate ligaments) — native-knee load sharing differs.
2. The medial force share of the OAI knees is drawn from 8 OrthoLoad subjects; alignment of the OAI knees is not in the open data.
3. Menisci carry the same share of the load in both compartments (κ is common to both plates).
4. KL0 knees are taken as the no-loss reference; cross-sectional differences are interpreted as loss.
5. "Eroded depth" (voxel matrix < 0.3) is the model counterpart of central thickness loss.
6. Ji et al.'s stage is matched to the ABM's local matrix content bins; marker detection is binary (≥ 1 UMI).
7. Body weight does not enter stage 1: a common load level is calibrated, so BMI effects are not tested here (stage 2).
